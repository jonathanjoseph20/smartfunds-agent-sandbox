# Operational Proof Narrative (Internal)

## What This Proves
This demo demonstrates a deterministic Code Factory loop:
- Policy gates are enforced consistently through governance tooling.
- Changes are small, scoped, and test-backed.
- Human oversight is preserved, including tier-based review and approval gates.

## Why It Matters for Regulated Infrastructure
Regulated issuance requires demonstrable controls and predictable behavior. A deterministic loop reduces risk by:
- Making changes auditable and repeatable.
- Preventing unreviewed or out-of-policy changes from shipping.
- Ensuring evidence is captured for each change (risk tier, tests, determinism statement).

## Operational Confidence
By running the three-cycle plan, we show:
- A feature can ship safely under Tier 1 constraints.
- Refactors do not alter behavior or integrity of the system.
- Bug fixes include regression tests and move through Tier 2 scrutiny.

This builds a clear, credible signal that the platform can evolve without compromising compliance or operational safety.
